## Overview

This repository contains our proposed solution for **Asian Paints Alchemy 2026**, where our team was selected among the **Top 92 participants from over 11,000 applicants nationwide**.

The project proposes an **Adaptive Self-Cleaning Coating (CASRE)** that combines materials engineering, sustainable chemistry, and business strategy to develop a premium architectural coating capable of reducing dust adhesion, improving stain removal, and lowering long-term maintenance costs.

---

## Project Objectives

- Design a fluorine-free adaptive self-cleaning coating.
- Improve both interior stain resistance and exterior dirt-release performance.
- Ensure compatibility with existing acrylic paint manufacturing infrastructure.
- Evaluate technical feasibility, scalability, and commercial viability.

---

## Key Features

- Adaptive acrylic–siloxane hybrid coating concept.
- Reversible amphiphilic surface reconstruction mechanism.
- Fluorine-free (PFAS-free) formulation.
- Smooth, recoatable coating surface.
- Compatible with existing water-based acrylic paint systems.
- Suitable for both interior and exterior architectural applications.

---

## Technical Highlights

- Literature benchmarking against:
  - Superhydrophobic silica coatings
  - Fluoropolymer coatings
  - TiO₂ photocatalytic coatings
  - Silicone-modified architectural paints

- Proposed processing strategy including:
  - Seeded emulsion polymerization
  - Siloxane functional monomer grafting
  - Pigment and filler dispersion
  - Functional additive incorporation
  - Final formulation and quality control

- Performance targets:
  - Reduced dust adhesion
  - Rain-triggered self-cleaning
  - ≥85–90% stain removal after cleaning
  - Improved scrub resistance
  - Extended repaint cycle (5–7 years)

## Scalability Strategy

The proposed coating is designed to be compatible with conventional architectural paint manufacturing.

Scale-up roadmap includes:

- Laboratory validation
- Pilot-scale production
- Commercial-scale manufacturing

No significant plant modifications or additional reactor designs are required.
